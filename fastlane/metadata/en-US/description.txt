You have two signals, one small traffic island and a flock waiting at the curb. Give the hens a gap, bring them to the island, then clear the second lane before the next queue builds. A tractor takes longer to clear than a car. At the station, a train timetable changes when you can cross.

Work through 36 shifts across the farm, market, mill and station. Early shifts teach you to move a flock one way. Later crossings send birds in both directions and make you share a shrinking island. A fresh daily shift is calculated on your device, even when you have no signal.

Pause a shift and return to it later. At the end, check how many hens made it home, how long birds and traffic waited and how far the queues backed up. Scrub through the busiest few seconds of the replay, then try the same timetable again. Your best results stay on your iPhone or iPad.
